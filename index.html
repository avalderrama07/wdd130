Perú

Peru is a country in South America. It has diverse landscapes and cultures, as well as some of the best cuisine in the world. Dishes such as ceviche, lomo saltado, causa, and pollo a la brasa, among others, are the most popular. It also has tourist attractions such as Machu Picchu, the Mountain of Seven Colors, the Nazca Lines, and the Historic Center of Lima.
